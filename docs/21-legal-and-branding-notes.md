# 21 – Legal and Branding Notes

You asked for the "exact same" design. These notes keep the project safe and professional.

## What you can replicate
- General layout and information architecture (header, mega menu, product grid, filters, PDP, cart drawer). These are common e-commerce patterns.
- Types of sections and UX behaviour.

## What you must not copy
- The company's **name, logo, trademarks, badges text** (e.g. their "Exclusive" / "Luxe" sub-brands) as official marks → use generic or your own names.
- **Photographs**, illustrations, icons and videos.
- **Product names, descriptions, and marketing copy.**
- Source code, CSS and proprietary fonts scraped from their site.
- Do not present the site as affiliated with, or an official store of, that company.

## Recommended approach
- Original brand name and logo.
- Original colour theme (docs/03).
- Licensed or generated images (docs/14).
- Original product copy.
- Footer disclaimer for demo builds: "Demo store for portfolio purposes. Not affiliated with any retailer. No real orders are processed."

## If this will be a real business
- Add real Terms, Privacy Policy, Returns and Shipping policies.
- Add a cookie/consent banner if analytics or marketing tags are used.
- Real payments require a payment provider and a backend/serverless function (out of scope here).
- Financing language must reflect real, compliant offers; the demo text is non-binding placeholder.
